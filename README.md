# Clark: AI-Native Execution for SMEs

**From operational blind spots to AI-native execution in 30 days.**

## 🚀 The Vision
Clark is an enterprise-grade AI platform designed specifically for SME and mid-market organizations. We provide a high-trust, explainable intelligence layer that solves real business problems through domain-specific agents and rapid operational diagnostics.

## 🛠️ Core Components
* **Operational X-Rays:** 2-week diagnostic scans to identify operational "bleeding" and bottlenecks.
* **Specialized AI Agents:** Autonomous workforce for HR, Marketing, and Procurement handling 80% of manual tasks.
* **AI Labs & Accelerators:** Plug-and-play building blocks to document and automate processes instantly.

## 🎨 Identity & UX
* **Design System:** High-trust aesthetic using the "Sunset Skies" palette (Deep Purples, Corals, and Gold).
* **Tech Stack:** Built with React, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion for premium, high-performance interactions.
* **Referential Design:** Inspired by industry leaders like CrewAI, Scale, and Linear.

## 📈 Value Proposition
* **Execution-First:** Immediate value with plug-and-play enablement.
* **Affordable Intelligence:** Enterprise-level reasoning at a fraction of the cost.
* **Explainable Governance:** Full transparency in AI decision-making.
